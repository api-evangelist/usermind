# Usermind

> Usermind was a Seattle-based journey / experience orchestration platform that captured and
> analyzed contextual data across customer-journey touchpoints. It was named a Leader in The
> Forrester Wave: Journey Orchestration Platforms, Q2 2020. Qualtrics announced its acquisition
> of Usermind on July 20, 2021; usermind.com now redirects to qualtrics.com and no independent
> Usermind API surface remains.

## Status

- Acquired by Qualtrics (announced 2021-07-20): https://www.qualtrics.com/news/qualtrics-acquires-usermind/
- Former website (now redirects to the acquirer): https://usermind.com
